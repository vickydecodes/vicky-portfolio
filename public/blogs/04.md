# balancing vision and execution

every developer starts with a vision of what they want to build. we see the finished product, the clean diagrams, and the satisfied users. but vision is easy. execution is the hard, daily, repetitive work of sitting down and writing code.

if you focus too much on the vision, you get stuck in planning phases. if you focus too much on execution, you end up writing code that doesn't align with where the product needs to go.

### the strategy at cookie

when building cookie, the strategy is simple: keep the overhead low, ship features in increments, and verify everything with tests. 

to maintain this balance, i keep a single list of core objectives that fits on one page. every ticket i work on must directly connect to one of those objectives. if a feature doesn't serve a core goal, it gets pushed back.

this prevents scope creep and keeps the engineering loop focused.

### small, deliberate steps

progress doesn't happen in huge leaps. it happens in small, daily commits. the key to execution is discipline — shipping a small update every day, even when it feels like progress is slow.

maintain a clear direction, focus on the immediate next step, and build cleanly.
