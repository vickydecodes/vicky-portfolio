# the power of saying no

no is one of the most underrated words in building products.

every yes to the wrong project, the misaligned client, the low-leverage task is a quiet no to the thing that actually matters.

![abstract conceptual image representing prioritization and limits](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?q=80&w=1172&auto=format&fit=crop&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&ixid=M3wxMjA3fDB8MHxwaG90by1wYWdlfHx8fGVufDB8fHx8fA%3D%3D)

### protecting your focus

saying no isn't arrogance. it's prioritisation with some self-awareness attached.

it protects time. it protects energy. it keeps direction intact.

clarity is a competitive advantage. and clarity almost always starts with what you're willing to refuse.
